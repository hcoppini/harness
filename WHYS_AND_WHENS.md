## 4. Root Cause Diagnosis (The Unseen Flaws)

### Flaw A: The Architect's Ego Trap (System as Dopamine Substitute)
Building Harness was cognitively easier and emotionally safer than solving 20 difficult parametric math problems or memorizing Polish chemistry nomenclature. Building an OS yields immediate visual competence; confronting academic gaps yields temporary feelings of stupidity. The system became an armor to protect the ego from confronting the 2.60 baseline.

### Flaw B: The Horizon Fallacy ("The US Reset")
Believing that an upcoming 2-week trip to the United States will act as a "clean slate" is a classic procrastination loop. No external environment fixes internal cognitive friction. Treating the trip as a reset grants subconscious permission to abandon rigor during the immediate 4-day critical window.

### Flaw C: The "What-the-Hell" Effect of Fragile Perfectionism
When an ambitious plan expects 100% compliance across 7 vectors (Math R, CS, German, Boxing, Gym, SIGG, School GPA), a single failure (e.g., missing German or burning an NP) triggers total system disengagement. Sustainable top performance is built on **unbreakable floors**, not brittle ceilings.

### Flaw D: Passive Absorption vs. Active Retrieval
Pretending to study occurs when the task lacks an immediate binary feedback loop (right vs. wrong). Reading notes and watching explanations feels productive but produces zero retention. Real academic mastery requires active problem solving where error is encountered immediately and resolved.
